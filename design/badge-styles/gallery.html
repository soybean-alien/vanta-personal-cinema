Vanta · badge styles

15 original treatments, each shown for Patron (golden), Night Owl (Rare) and Marathoner (Epic). The chosen style is the badge you earn, pin and see on the unlock ticket.
01Rosette Medal

Today’s look: a scalloped metal rosette around a black face. Classic award, a little formal.

02Enamel Pin

A hard-enamel lapel pin: polished metal outline, flat glossy color, one shine. Feels collectible and pinnable.

03Reel Coin

A coin whose rim is film sprockets, struck in the rarity color. Unmistakably cinema.

04Deco Crest

An art-deco picture-palace crest: stepped shield, sunburst fan, gold pinstripes.

05Neon Sign

The emblem as glowing neon tubing on a black plaque, the way theatre signs glow at night.

06Wax Seal

A pressed wax seal with an embossed emblem and two ribbon tails. Personal, like a signed letter.

07Ticket Stub

The badge is itself a tiny admission ticket, matching the unlock toast.

08Aperture Coin

A camera aperture whose blades are the rarity color, opening onto the emblem.

09Holo Hexagon

A faceted hexagon with a holographic shimmer: modern, gamer-friendly, collectible-card energy.

10Embroidered Patch

A sewn fabric patch with a stitched border: cozy, crafty, like a jacket patch.

11Marquee Bulbs

A black disc ringed with glowing marquee bulbs, the emblem lit in the middle. Old Hollywood.

12Monoline

Minimal: a thin double ring and a single-weight line emblem. The calmest, most Apple-like option.

13Cut Gem

A faceted gemstone in the rarity color with the emblem etched on the table facet. Rarity as a jewel.

14Vinyl Record

A black vinyl disc with grooves and a colored center label. Media nostalgia.

15Laurel Award

The emblem in a circle framed by laurel branches: a festival-winner feel ("Official Selection").
