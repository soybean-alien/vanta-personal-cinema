Vanta · icon concepts

Each card shows the icon large, then at 48, 32 and 16 px (taskbar and desktop sizes) on a black and a light background.

Void

01
Event Horizon
A perfect black disc rimmed by the last ring of light. The purest reading of the name.
02
Diamond Ring
Total eclipse with the diamond-ring flash on the edge. One spark against the black.
03
Crescent
A sliver of gold escaping a black disc, like light grazing the edge of something that absorbs everything.
04
Absorption
Concentric rings get brighter as they fall inward, then stop dead at the black core.
05
Light Sink
Twelve rays converge on a black core and vanish. Light goes in and never comes out.
06
Singularity
A black hole with a tilted accretion disc: the back half bends over the top and the front cuts across.
07
Swatch
A square of vantablack set into a lighter dark tile, with a tiny gold maker’s mark. A materials-lab feel.
08
Iris
An eye’s iris in warm gold around a black pupil, with one catchlight. Built for watching.

Monogram

09
Blade V
A single thin geometric V with mitred strokes. Minimal and sharp, close to a fashion label.
10
Negative V
A V cut out of a gold coin. Negative space does the work, and it stays readable at 16 px.
11
Chevron Play
A V turned on its side becomes a play arrow: the letter and the action in one mark.
12
Split V
Two strokes that nearly meet, in two tones of gold. The gap at the point is where the light disappears.
13
Facet V
The V as a cut gem: two lit facets meeting at a ridge.
14
Didone V
High-contrast serif V (thick and thin strokes, hairline serifs) in champagne. The luxury-magazine take.
15
Ring V
A thin V inside a thin circle. Quiet, badge-like and symmetrical.
16
Ribbon V
Two bands crossing at the point like a folded ribbon, the back band in shadow.
17
Convergence
Two beams of light fade in from the top and meet in a hot point: a V drawn by light itself.
18
Crest
A heraldic shield with a V. Old-money and a little regal.

Cinema

19

20
Film Gate
A frame of film with sprocket holes and nothing in the frame: black, waiting for the picture.
21
Curtain Call
Stage curtains part to reveal pure black, and the gap between them forms the V.
22
Reel
A classic film reel reduced to its outline. Instantly reads as movies.
23
Play Void
A gold disc with a black play button punched through it. The universal symbol, made dark.
24
Afterglow
A widescreen of pure black with warm light bleeding around its edges, like a cinema just before the film starts.
25
Marquee
A V spelled in marquee bulbs. Old Hollywood with a soft glow.
26
Spotlight
A single cone of light falls on an empty stage. Black everywhere else.
27
Lens
A camera lens front with deep glass and a cold reflection. Technical and premium.
